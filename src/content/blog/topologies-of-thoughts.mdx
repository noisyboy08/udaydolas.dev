## Overview

**Topologies of Thoughts** is an immersive knowledge graph platform that lets you literally map the topology of your thoughts. Build living knowledge graphs from notes, explore them in full 3D with camera and hand gestures, and route every AI call through your own self-hosted FastAPI backend — so your API keys never leave the server.

The platform fundamentally reimagines how humans organize ideas: instead of linear notes in a bullet list or flat folders in a file system, every concept becomes a **node** in a spatial 3D graph that you can physically navigate with gestures, zoom into with pinch motions, and orbit around with wrist movements.

![Topologies of Thoughts Preview](/images/blog/Topologies of Thoughts.png)

## The Problem it Solves

Human knowledge is inherently non-linear and deeply interconnected — yet every mainstream note-taking tool forces ideas into linear hierarchies (folders, bullets, headers). The best thinkers work in webs: ideas connect across topics, cascade into each other, and form clusters of meaning that can't be captured in a flat list. Topologies of Thoughts is the first note-taking environment that matches the actual topology of human thought.

## Key Features

### 🌐 3D Living Knowledge Graph

Every note, idea, and concept you add becomes a node in a three-dimensional graph. Connections between ideas become edges with computed semantic weight values — the floating decimal numbers visible in the hero (0.1456, 0.3543, 0.3189) represent the semantic similarity score between connected concepts, calculated by the AI backend. The entire graph renders in real-time 3D using Three.js with a particle-density background that creates a visceral sense of navigating idea-space.

### 🤲 Hand & Camera Gesture Navigation

Navigate your knowledge graph without touching a mouse or keyboard. The gesture system uses WebXR Device API with camera-based hand tracking:

- **Pinch** with one hand to select and drag a node
- **Two-handed pinch** to zoom in and out of the graph
- **Wrist rotation** to orbit the 3D camera around the graph's center
- **Navigate concepts as nodes and edges, pinch to select, two hands to zoom, wrist to orbit** — the in-app tooltip in the hero summarizes the full gesture vocabulary.

### 🔗 Live Graph Mode

A live streaming graph view shows ideas and connections forming in real-time as you type or dictate notes. As you write about a concept, the AI backend identifies related nodes already in your graph and draws animated edge connections in real-time. Watching your knowledge graph grow live creates an entirely new relationship with thinking and note-taking.

### 🤖 Self-Hosted AI Backend (FastAPI)

Every AI feature routes through a FastAPI Python backend that the user deploys on their own infrastructure:

- **Note summarization** — Condenses long notes into a concise node label
- **Concept linking** — Suggests new edges between semantically related nodes
- **Semantic similarity scoring** — Computes the edge weight values displayed on connections
- **Graph Q&A** — Ask questions about your knowledge graph; the AI reasons over connected nodes to answer

Your OpenAI, Groq, or Anthropic API keys live on your server — never in the browser, never in a third-party database.

### 🔍 Semantic Edge Discovery

The AI backend continuously computes pairwise semantic similarity between all nodes in your graph and surfaces non-obvious connections you hadn't made explicitly. These suggested edges appear as dashed lines that you can confirm or dismiss — helping you discover hidden relationships between ideas across different topics in your knowledge base.

## Design Philosophy

The visual identity is deliberate minimalism in service of cognitive clarity — a pure black canvas with white nodes and teal-green weighted edge labels. No color distractions from the content. The particle-field background creates depth without competing with the graph structure. Typography is clean, technical, and compact — this is a thinking tool, not a marketing website.
