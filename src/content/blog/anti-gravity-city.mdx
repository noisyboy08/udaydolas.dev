## Overview

**Anti-Gravity-City** transforms static GitHub repositories into a living, breathing 3D Cyberpunk Metropolis. Instead of reading flat file trees or wall-to-wall diff logs, developers can now _fly through_ their codebase — each file becomes a glowing skyscraper, each import/export dependency becomes a neon energy beam connecting buildings at different altitude layers.

The project was born from a simple but powerful idea: **codebases are architectural cities in disguise**. Complex systems have districts (modules), highways (data flows), and skylines (abstraction layers). Anti-Gravity-City makes that metaphor physically visible and interactive.

![Anti-Gravity-City Preview](/images/blog/Anti-Gravity.png)

## The Problem it Solves

For developers working on large codebases, understanding the overall architecture at a glance is nearly impossible. You either read documentation (usually outdated), trace imports manually, or rely on someone senior to explain the structure. Anti-Gravity-City eliminates all three pain points by making the architecture _literally visible_ as a 3D cityscape you can fly through and interact with.

## Key Features

### 🏙️ 3D City Renderer

Each source file is mapped to a building. File size determines building height, folder nesting sets the altitude tier, and import/export relationships draw glowing connector beams between structures. The entire city rebuilds live as you push new commits — you can literally watch your codebase grow.

### 🛡️ Force Field Districts

Related modules and folders are enclosed in interactive force field bubbles with distinct color palettes, making it immediately clear which parts of the codebase belong together. Hover a district to see all its member files and inter-dependencies highlighted.

### 🤖 Gemini AI Codebase Chatbot

A fully embedded Google Gemini AI assistant reads your parsed repository structure and answers architectural questions like "What does this module depend on?" or "Which files have the most coupling?" — directly inside the 3D control panel. No context switching, no documentation hunting.

### ⏱️ Git Timeline Time-Travel

Scrub through a commit timeline slider and watch the city rebuild itself, building by building, tracking exactly how the repository grew from a single file to a full metropolis. You can pause, rewind, and replay the entire history of your codebase as a cinematic 3D animation.

### 🎵 Procedural Synth Engine

All UI interactions, hover events, camera movements, and city transitions are accompanied by a real-time **Web Audio API synthesizer** generating immersive cyberpunk ambient sound — no pre-recorded audio files. The engine synthesizes tones, hums, and pulses dynamically based on what you interact with.

## Design Philosophy

The visual identity is pure cyberpunk noir — deep space blacks, electric neon blues and purples, glowing amber building windows, and procedural particle systems that fill the sky. Every element was chosen to reinforce the idea that you are piloting through a living, breathing digital city.
