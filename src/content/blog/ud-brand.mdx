The UD brand is built on a clean, technical visual identity inspired by blueprints, pixel art, and architectural geometry.

### Core Visual Elements

1. **The UD Mark**: A minimal, blocky representation of the initials "U" and "D" that serves as the primary favicon and app icon.
2. **The Isometric Logotype**: A 3D-extruded isometric representation of the "UD" initials, designed to look like an architectural blueprint structure.
3. **The Pixel-Art Wordmark**: A flat, uniform-height wordmark signature of "UDAY DOLAS" that spans the footer of the site, featuring a spotlight cursor-reveal mask effect.
